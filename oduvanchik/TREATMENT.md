# «Одуванчик» — 10 s, Watercolor Brush

Brief (defaults filled in): style Watercolor Brush; topic chosen by the director: a dandelion;
10 s, 1920×1080, 60 fps (the style's frame rate); no narration, music + foley only; on-screen text in Russian;
no storyboard stop.

## Three candidate structures
1. **Change in time on a locked sheet** — a specimen plate: the plant paints itself in, flowers, turns to a seed
   clock; a gust lifts the seeds off the page. Before/after in one view, then release.
2. **A single journey** — follow one seed across a meadow transect from the plant to where it lands.
   Too much ground for 10 s; the seed would be a dot.
3. **A list that turns** — three journal specimens (bud, flower, seed clock) side by side, the last one blows away.
   Reads as a slide; three labels can't be read in 10 s.

**Chosen: 1, ending with a short lateral drift after the seeds (a taste of 2).** One subject, one turn
(yellow → white), and the release is the native move: the painted puff breaks into strokes that fly.

## Logline and arc
A dandelion paints itself onto the journal page, goes to seed, and the wind takes every seed but one.
Setup (plant appears) → turn (flower becomes a seed clock; a breath of silence) → ending (the gust;
seeds drift right; a quiet handwritten line).

## Benchmark
Naturalist field-journal plates and nature-documentary time-lapse of a dandelion going to seed.
Learn: specimen + note layout, structure before detail, the held breath before the release.
Don't take: any specific plate, shot or melody.

## Beat sheet
- 0.0 blank warm paper; the first brush rustle. 0.2–1.6 stem paints bottom→top, leaves (rosette) 0.8–2.0.
- 1.6–2.8 the yellow flower head blooms, petal strokes outward (burst-ish, on beats). 
- 2.0–4.6 hand note «Taraxacum officinale» writes in with leader line to the flower (≥ 2.6 s visible).
- 3.4–4.6 painted transformation: yellow cross-fades to the white seed clock (grey-blue pappus strokes).
- 4.6–5.6 near-silence: music drops out, only paper/room. Push in.
- 5.6 gust (whoosh, first sound after silence); seeds detach and fly up-right in a stream; camera tracks right.
- 6.6–10.0 handwritten line «до следующей весны» paints in near the drifting seeds (3.4 s held); one seed
  drifts last; brush lifted.

## Cue map
72 bpm (0.833 s/beat), 3/4 feel. Kalimba + soft glockenspiel + felt-ish pad (synthesised plucks/bells).
- Bars 1–2 (0–5.0): kalimba arpeggio, sparse; bloom notes on beats at 1.67, 2.50.
- 4.6–5.6: music out (silence).
- 5.6: gust; music returns, higher register, glock sparkles following the seeds; final chord 8.3, ring out to 10.
