# Facts and numbers on screen
Topic choice: Vsauce "What If Everyone JUMPED At Once?" had 24,765,085 views when cited in a study (ResearchGate,
"Science Popularization Videos by Independent YouTube Creators…") — the biggest confirmed "what if" number found.

- «8,2 млрд адам»: world population 2026 ≈ 8.24 bn (PRB 2026 World Population Data Sheet) – 8.30 bn (UN WPP 2024).
- «≈ 0,41 млрд тонна»: 8.2 bn × 50 kg average (children included) = 4.1·10¹¹ kg; consistent with total human biomass
  estimates (Bar-On, Phillips & Milo, PNAS 2018: ~0.06 Gt carbon ≈ 0.4 Gt wet mass).
- «Жер андан 15 триллион эсе оор»: Earth 5.972·10²⁴ kg / 4.1·10¹¹ kg = 1.46·10¹³.
- «40 см секирсе … абада 0,57 секунд»: t = 2·√(2h/g) = 2·√(0.8/9.81) = 0.57 s.
- «Жер 0,00000000000003 м ылдый жылат — атомдон 3 600 эсе аз»: momentum conservation, Earth moves (m/M)·h =
  4.1e11/5.972e24 × 0.40 m = 2.75·10⁻¹⁴ m; an atom ≈ 1·10⁻¹⁰ m → ~3 600× smaller. The chart's red curve is this
  motion magnified ×10¹³ (labelled on screen).
- «Ысык-Көл · 6 236 км²»: standard surface area of Lake Issyk-Kul. «1 м² × 8,2 млрд = 8 200 км²» → 1.3×.
- «учактар 1 жылда ~5 млрд жүргүнчү»: IATA — 4.99 bn passengers in 2025 (June 2025 estimate), 5.2 bn forecast for 2026.
  «≈ 1,6 жыл»: 8.2 / 5 = 1.64 (counts every passenger seat of every airline, ignoring that most trips are round trips).
