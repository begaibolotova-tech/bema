# «Бүт адамзат бир убакта секирсе эмне болот?» — 60 s Instagram Reel, Data Storytelling

Brief: Kyrgyz; 1080×1920, 30 fps; topic chosen from research (Vsauce's "What If Everyone JUMPED At Once?" had
24.7 M views — the biggest confirmed number for a "what if" video we found without vidIQ). Voice: ElevenLabs
(eleven_v3, voice «Жусуп Кыргыз»), but the account had 83 credits: hook + verdict voiced (71 credits); the
facts are told by the chart's handwritten notes. When credits are topped up, the full narration replaces it.

## Three candidate structures
1. **One dot = 100 million people (chosen):** a unit chart of 82 dots lands (humanity), → mass vs the Earth →
   the jump as a height-vs-time chart where the Earth's line is flat until a 10¹³× punch-in reveals its tiny
   bump → where would we stand (area vs Ysyk-Köl) → the real problem: getting home (flights per year) → verdict.
2. A countdown of five facts: an infographic, not a story.
3. Before/after slope (Earth before and after the jump): a single beat, too thin for a minute.

## Arc
Setup: 8.2 bn people (the unit chart) → turn: everyone jumps; the Earth's line stays flat — until we zoom ten
trillion times → ending: the jump is harmless, getting home is the real problem; «Жер муну байкабай да калат».

## Data (see FACTS.md)
8.2 bn people; ~0.41 bn t (avg 50 kg incl. children); Earth 5.97·10²⁴ kg → ~15 trillion× heavier; jump 0.4 m →
0.57 s in the air; Earth moves ~0.00000000000003 m (≈3 600× smaller than an atom); 1 m² each → 8 200 km² vs
Ysyk-Köl 6 236 km²; airlines ~5 bn passengers/year → ~1.6 years of all flights to bring everyone home.
