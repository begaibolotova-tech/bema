# «Одуванчик. Круг» — 60 s, Watercolor Brush

Brief: style Watercolor Brush; topic (director's choice, the user had no preference): the year of one dandelion seed;
60 s, 1920×1080, 60 fps. Narration was planned (ElevenLabs, Russian) but the account had too few credits; edge-tts is unreachable here
and Kokoro has no Russian. So: no voice; the facts go into hand-written notes (the style's specimen + note grammar).
No subtitles (no speech). No storyboard stop. The 10 s film «Одуванчик» is the seed of this one.

## Three candidate structures
1. **A journal kept over one year**: locked pages, spring/summer/autumn/winter, each painted over the last.
   Static; loses the flight, which is the best moment we have.
2. **One continuous painted world, one seed's journey (chosen)**: the plant goes to seed, the wind takes one seed;
   the camera follows it in one lateral dolly across meadow, river and village; it lands, the locked view runs
   through autumn and winter, a new dandelion grows in spring, goes to seed again. A circle in one oner.
3. **Anatomy of a seed clock**: push from the head into one seed, labelled parts (achene, beak, pappus, vortex).
   Good facts, little story; we keep one specimen-note beat of it inside structure 2.

Chosen: 2, with one specimen beat from 3. Opening: blank paper painting itself into a flower. Ending: the new head
lets go its seeds the same way, the title, the page held — the circle closes on the same image it opened with.

## Logline and arc
A dandelion seed is carried off by the wind, waits out a winter in the ground and becomes a dandelion again.
Setup (flower → seed clock, 0–14) → journey (14–33) → turn: landing, waiting through autumn and winter (33–44)
→ ending: spring, a new flower, seeds go again, title (44–60).

## Benchmark
Naturalist field journals; time-lapse nature documentaries of seed dispersal.
Learn: one hero, structure-before-detail, the hold before release, seasons on a locked view.
Don't take: specific plates, shots, music.
