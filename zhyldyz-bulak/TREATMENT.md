# «Жылдыз булак» — treatment

Style: **Crayon Picture Book** (`crayon-book`). 1920×1080, 24 fps, ≈5 min. Language: Kyrgyz.
Voice: **none** — no Kyrgyz TTS is reachable here (Kokoro has no Kyrgyz, edge-tts is blocked). The narration is the
book's printed words, written in on the page; music rises to carry the film (the style allows a music-led film).
Font: Caveat (OFL) — the style's Patrick Hand has no Cyrillic; Caveat is a handwriting face with ң ө ү.

## 1. Three structures

1. **A flip through a picture book** — one page per stage of the journey (village → forest → river → fog → spring → home),
   each page drawn while we read it, joined by page turns. Natural for a 5-minute story with 12 stages.
2. **One long page, one long camera** — the whole journey is a single map-like page that draws itself ahead of the
   children as the camera trucks up the mountain; home at the bottom, the spring at the top.
3. **Two crayons, one page** — Aizada's red and Nurbek's blue draw alternately; they argue in the fog and combine.

**Chosen: 2 inside 1.** A book of pages (time jumps, chapters, reading rhythm for 5 minutes of text), but the journey
pages (forest → river → mountain → fog) are *one tall page* the camera climbs, so the middle of the film is one
continuous ascent, and the light of the little creature is literally what draws the path ahead (**drawn live**).
The peak uses **wax resist**: the Star Spring, its reflections and the stars are drawn in white crayon on blank paper,
invisible, and the night-blue wash reveals them. The ending echoes it: years later the walnut tree's lights are white wax
revealed by the last wash; «Аягы» is written in the sky, and the camera pulls back past the page edge: the book lies open
on grandma's felt shyrdak.

**Length.** The printed words set the pace (each block stays ≥ letters ÷ 15 + 1.5 s), so the film runs 5 min 16 s.

## 2. Logline and arc

A girl and her little brother help a lost star-creature climb home to the Star Spring, and learn that a friend's light
grows from kindness — even when it means letting go.

- Setup (p1–4): the village, the lost creature Жылдызча, grandma's blessing.
- Journey (p5–8): forest play, the river (courage), the creature fades (kindness), fog and fear → the song relights it.
- Turn (p9): the Star Spring revealed by the wash.
- Ending (p10–12): farewell and a golden walnut; home; years later the tree full of lights.

## 3. Benchmark

- A hand-drawn winter picture-book film (1980s): learn the tenderness, held frames that breathe, one tune carrying a
  wordless passage. Don't take characters, the song, snow, flight.
- Central-Asian felt-applique and shyrdak patterns: learn the ornament grammar (ram's-horn curls) for the dress hem,
  the carpet and the title frame. Don't copy a specific carpet.

## 4. Characters & crayon box

- **Айзада** (9): two black braids, red dress, gold ornament hem (mustard), black vest.
- **Нурбек** (7): white ak-kalpak (paper white + plum outline + black brim), blue vest, brown trousers.
- **Жылдызча**: tiny round silvery-blue creature, huge golden eyes, long ringed tail (blue/white rings) whose tip glows
  (lemon + white wax star).
- **Чоң апа**: white headscarf, plum dress.
- Box (12): indigo line, red, mustard, lemon, sky blue, cornflower, grass, olive, brown, peach (skin), rose (cheeks),
  white. Wash: **ultramarine** (night) only.

## 7. Cue map

Waltz lilt in 3/4, 84 bpm (bar ≈ 2.14 s). Theme on recorder (flute), ukulele/nylon-guitar picking, music box,
glockenspiel note per lit stone and per revealed star, bassoon for the comic tumble, clarinet long notes in fog.

- Title: music box alone, low-passed, the theme's first phrase.
- p1–p4: guitar picking + recorder theme fragments; page turns land on a downbeat.
- Forest: full lilt, shaker; Nurbek's tumbles = bassoon blips.
- River: drop to guitar pulse; glockenspiel note on each lit stone.
- Kalpak/bread: clarinet long notes, sparse.
- Fog: **silence 1** (music stops, only wind) → the song: recorder alone sings the theme → glock sparkle as the tail relights.
- Spring: **silence 2** (one breath) → full theme heard once, the only full statement, celesta/glock per star.
- Farewell → home: music box, slower; resolves on the tonic as the walnut is planted.
- Ending: theme's last phrase, music box winding down with a pitch droop under the pull-out.
